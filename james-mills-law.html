About the practice

01 — ABOUT
Attorney

James Mills

  • ExperienceNearly 20 years
  • Licensed inOR, WA, CA
  • FocusBusiness & Real Estate

Nearly two decades in, James Mills still sees the job the same way: helping people and businesses work through problems, big and small.

Licensed in Oregon, Washington, and California, James guides clients through the kinds of conflicts modern life tends to produce — disputes involving family members, business partners, or other third parties. He advises entrepreneurs on starting and running their businesses, and helps clients through the real estate transactions that come with buying, selling, or holding property. As a trial lawyer, his practice also includes advocating for clients in personal injury and business litigation matters.
